Use the uploaded logo as the primary brand identity. Preserve its emerald green and silver colour palette and ensure the logo appears elegant with generous spacing.

Premium Feel

Every section should feel like entering a luxury jewellery showroom.

Use generous whitespace, cinematic photography, subtle glow effects, elegant typography, and refined animations. Avoid crowded layouts. The website should create an emotional impression of exclusivity, trust, and timeless beauty.

Make the final design visually stunning, modern, and premium enough that it could compete with India's top jewellery brand websites while maintaining the unique identity of Maragatha Mayil Jewels.
